About

I am a PhD student in computational psycholinguistics and machine learning. My research looks at human-inspired sentence processing in (large) language models: how well do these models align with human cognition, and can they help us understand human language and language impairments?

I work at the Laboratoire de Linguistique Formelle (LLF), CNRS, at Université Paris Cité, supervised by Benoît Crabbé and Guillaume Wisniewski. From January 2025 to January 2026 I was a visiting researcher in the COLT group at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona.

My PhD is part of the ANR-funded project COMPO. With other research groups, we explore how to build inductive biases for compositionality and memory constraints into language models, so that they need less data and fewer computational resources.
